Our Story

Heritage, Reimagined

House of Motubatsi is a Basotho-inspired luxury fashion brand that celebrates heritage, identity and modern elegance. We create timeless designs that blend traditional Basotho culture with contemporary style.

Our brand represents pride, confidence, sophistication and appreciation for Basotho heritage — carried from the mountains of Lesotho onto the global stage.
