About Us

About Motubatsi Luxury

Motubatsi Luxury is a Basotho-inspired luxury fashion concept created to celebrate the beauty and significance of Basotho heritage through contemporary fashion.

Meet the Team

Founder & CEO

Meet the founder and CEO of Motubatsi Luxury, the creative mind behind our Basotho-inspired luxury fashion brand.

Motubatsi Luxury was created to celebrate Basotho heritage, identity and culture through modern luxury fashion.

The founder's vision is to create timeless designs that bring together traditional Basotho influences and contemporary elegance.

Why We Exist

Inspiration, Mission & Vision

Our Inspiration

The brand draws inspiration from cultural elements such as the Basotho blanket and mokorotlo (Sotho hat), combined with modern luxury aesthetics.

Our Mission

To preserve and celebrate Basotho cultural identity by presenting traditional inspiration in a modern, elegant and meaningful way.

Our Vision

To establish House of Motubatsi as a distinctive African luxury fashion brand that connects heritage with contemporary fashion.
